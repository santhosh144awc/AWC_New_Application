In Indian households, insurance has traditionally been purchased as a tax-saving or wealth-building instrument through traditional endowment plans or ULIPs. This fundamental confusion often leaves families **severely underinsured** while paying high annual premiums for minimal protection.

## The Golden Rule: Never Mix Insurance and Investment

* **Pure Term Life Insurance:** Provides focused income-replacement death benefit protection for dependents with substantial coverage (₹1 Crore to ₹2 Crore+) at affordable indicative annual cost (e.g. approximately ₹10,000–₹18,000/yr for a healthy 30-year-old non-smoking individual under standard underwriting, based on indicative 2026 market quotes). If the insured passes away during the term, the nominee receives the full sum assured.
* **Traditional Endowment / Money-Back Policy:** Combines small insurance cover with low returns (illustrative historical internal rate of return typically in the 4% to 5.5% range based on declared non-guaranteed participating bonuses), offering minimal cover (₹5–10 Lakhs) for expensive annual premiums (₹60,000–₹1,00,000/yr).

**The Better Strategy:** Secure adequate high-cover Pure Term Insurance to protect your family, and deploy your investment surplus into disciplined Equity Mutual Fund SIPs aligned with your multi-year financial goals.

## How Much Life Cover Does Your Family Need?

Using the **Needs-Based Human Life Value (HLV)** framework, your life cover should account for:
1. **Income Replacement:** 12 to 15 times your current annual take-home income.
2. **100% of Outstanding Debts:** Home loan, car loan, and personal liabilities so your family inherits zero debt.
3. **Future Crucial Milestones:** Children's higher education and marriage targets.
4. **Minus:** Existing liquid investments and current term life cover.

## Important Takeaways

1. Life insurance is designed to replace your economic earning capacity for your dependents if you are no longer there.
2. Separate pure risk protection from long-term wealth creation.
3. Ensure your term cover is sufficient (typically ₹1 Crore+) to clear all liabilities and sustain your family's living standard.
