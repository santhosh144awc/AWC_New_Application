In Indian households, insurance has traditionally been sold as a tax-saving or wealth-building instrument through traditional endowment plans or ULIPs. This fundamental confusion often leaves families **severely underinsured** and paying high annual premiums for minimal protection.

## The Golden Rule: Never Mix Insurance and Investment

* **Pure Term Life Insurance:** Provides 100% Financial Protection for Dependents with large coverage (₹1 Crore to ₹2 Crore+) at low annual cost (₹10,000–₹18,000/yr). If the insured passes away, the nominee receives the full ₹1–2 Crore sum.
* **Traditional Endowment / Money-Back Policy:** Mixes insurance and low returns (typically 4% to 5.5%), offering minimal cover (₹5–10 Lakhs) for expensive annual premiums (₹60,000–₹1,00,000/yr).

**The Better Strategy:** Buy a high-cover Pure Term Insurance policy at a low cost, and invest the remaining surplus into disciplined Equity Mutual Fund SIPs.

## How Much Life Cover Does Your Family Need?

Using the **Needs-Based Human Life Value (HLV)** framework, your life cover should cover:
1. **Income Replacement:** 12 to 15 times your current annual take-home income.
2. **100% of Outstanding Debts:** Home loan, car loan, and personal liabilities so your family inherits zero debt.
3. **Future Crucial Milestones:** Children education and marriage targets.
4. **Minus:** Existing liquid investments and current life cover.

## Important Takeaways

1. Life insurance is designed to replace your economic value to your dependents if you are no longer there.
2. Always buy Pure Term Insurance; never mix insurance with investments.
3. Ensure your cover is large enough (typically ₹1 Crore+) to clear all loans and sustain your family lifestyle.
