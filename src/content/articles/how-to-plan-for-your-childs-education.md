Every Indian parent dreams of giving their children the best possible education. However, education inflation in India historically runs between **8% to 10% per year**—significantly higher than standard headline consumer inflation.

A 4-year professional degree that costs ₹20 Lakhs today could easily cost ₹50 Lakhs to ₹75 Lakhs by the time a 3-year-old child enters college at age 18.

## The 3-Step Education Planning Blueprint

### Step 1: Quantify the Target in Future Rupees
Always adjust today course fee for inflation: Future Cost = Current Cost × (1 + Inflation)^Years. If an MBA costs ₹25,00,000 today, in 12 years at 8% education inflation, the required corpus will be approximately **₹62,95,420**.

### Step 2: Separate Long-Term Growth from Short-Term Needs
* **Horizon > 7 Years:** Allocate heavily towards Equity Mutual Funds (Flexi-Cap, Large & Mid-Cap) via monthly SIPs to beat educational inflation.
* **Horizon < 3 Years (Entering College Soon):** Gradually shift accumulated equity wealth into Low-Duration Debt Funds or Fixed Deposits via Systematic Transfer Plans (STP) to protect capital against sudden market downturns right before admission fees are due.

### Step 3: Protect the Goal with Pure Term Life Cover
If an earning parent passes away unexpectedly, a child education should not be derailed. Ensure the primary earning parent has adequate pure term life insurance equal to at least 15–20 times annual income plus specific liabilities and educational targets.

## Important Takeaways

1. Education inflation in India runs at 8%–10% annually; never plan with today cost alone.
2. Use disciplined monthly Equity SIPs when your child is young, and step up your investment as income grows.
3. De-risk your portfolio into debt instruments 2–3 years prior to college admission to lock in your accumulated corpus safely.
